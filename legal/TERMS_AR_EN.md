### 1. What is Wazifa?
Wazifa is a simple jobs website and app: any registered person can post a job, search and filter jobs, message other users inside the app, and save posts to review later. The app is in Arabic and English, and works in the browser and as an installed app.

- Register with a valid email address and a password of your own. You are responsible for keeping your password secret and for everything that happens in your account.
- One account per person. Do not register an account in someone else's name, and do not give your account to anyone else.
- The first account registered in the app is the owner's/admin account.

### 3. User responsibilities
- Give true information about yourself and about the jobs you post.
- Do not impersonate another person, company, or shop.
- Do not share your login details with anyone.
- Tell us immediately if you learn that someone else is using your account without your permission.

### 4. Prohibited content
The following are forbidden to post or send, and will be removed and may lead to account suspension:
- Fake or misleading jobs, or job offers that ask applicants to pay a fee or money in exchange for employment.
- Fraud of any kind, requests for money transfers, and suspicious links.
- Spam, unwanted advertising, and duplicate posts.
- Abusive, threatening, harassing, discriminatory, or hateful content.
- Sexual, violent, or illegal content.
- Any content that infringes the rights of others, including copyright and trademarks.
- Collecting other users' data or trying to access their accounts.

### 5. Job posting rules
- Post real jobs only, and write the company or shop name, city, job type, salary if any, description, and requirements honestly.
- People's email addresses are not shown publicly in the app.
- The application-email field has been removed from posting; contact with the poster is through the in-app message button.
- You can comment on posts, but comments are subject to the same prohibited-content rules.
- Posting is currently automatic by the owner's decision, but the admin reviews posts and may delete any violating post without prior notice.
- Photo/video upload in job posts is currently locked; only a profile picture in the user profile is allowed.

### 6. Chat rules
- In-app chat is for communication about jobs only.
- Do not send spam, repeated, or unwanted messages.
